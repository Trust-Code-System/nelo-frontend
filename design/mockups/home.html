The difference

Ready to wear, measured to you.

Most labels ask for a dress size and hope. We hold seven measurements to a tenth of a millimetre, keep them on your account, and cut against them — whether you are buying off the rail or commissioning a gown from nothing.

It is why our range runs to a UK 30 and why nothing here is sold in a range like “34–35”. A quarter inch is 6.35 millimetres. We record it that way.
