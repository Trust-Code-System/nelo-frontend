# Couture Portal

NELO's storefront uses a monochrome editorial shell whose soft arches contrast with a precise grid. Real garments and portraits carry every vivid colour.

## Signature

On a first home-page visit, two black curved curtains part after the NELO letters assemble at centre. The sequence is brief, skippable, session-scoped, and removed for reduced-motion users. The same split-letter wordmark rolls once on hover, keyboard focus, or touch.

## Surfaces

- White canvas, black type, stone secondary ground.
- 14–18px utility surfaces; 28–72px editorial arches.
- Hairline borders and restrained neutral shadows only.
- No gradients, glass effects, or decorative colour layers.

## Typography

Instrument Sans carries both display and interface copy. Large headings use tight tracking, controlled italics, and compact line-height; IBM Plex Mono is reserved for measurements, prices, and system readouts.

## Motion

- Interaction feedback: 120–260ms.
- Wordmark roll: 520ms with stagger.
- Scroll reveals: 700–900ms, once.
- Intro: approximately 2 seconds, played once per session.
- All motion honors `prefers-reduced-motion` and content is complete without JavaScript.
