# NELO WOMAN — Product Context

> These facts are inferred from the product repository, the live NELO storefront, NELO's public social presence, and the owner's explicit redesign brief. They are not invented testimonials or campaign claims.

## Product

NELO WOMAN is a Lagos fashion house selling ready-to-wear womenswear and accepting bespoke and bridal commissions. The storefront supports Nigerian and international markets, size guidance from UK 6–30, measurement profiles, account journeys, cart and checkout.

## Users

- Women looking for expressive, sculptural clothing rather than anonymous basics.
- Ready-to-wear customers who need a confident path from editorial discovery to product and fit information.
- Bespoke and bridal clients who need to understand the consultation, fitting, and commission process before making contact.

## Positioning

The house language is “Statement Femininity.” The brand should feel confident, feminine, exacting, and recognisably Lagos-made. It should read as a fashion house with commerce capability—not as a generic ecommerce template.

## Brand commitments

- Use NELO's real campaign and product photography. Photography is the source of colour.
- Keep the interface predominantly black, white, and quiet neutral.
- Treat fit and measurement precision as a differentiator, not as technical clutter.
- Never model bespoke or bridal work as an ordinary cart item.
- Preserve the `NELO WOMAN` name and the geometric character of the existing wordmark.

## Experience principles

1. **Editorial first, commerce clear.** The page may feel cinematic, but prices, navigation, sizing, and calls to action stay legible.
2. **One memorable gesture.** The curtain-to-wordmark entrance is the authored focal motion; supporting motion remains brief and functional.
3. **Curves with intent.** Rounded and arched frames evoke portals, mirrors, and garment silhouettes; controls remain compact and precise.
4. **Accessible by construction.** Keyboard, touch, reduced-motion, responsive layouts, and semantic landmarks are first-class requirements.
5. **No borrowed identity.** References inform interaction grammar only. NELO's imagery, words, and proportions make the result specific.

## Technical constraints

- Next.js App Router with React Server Components and small client-side interaction islands.
- GSAP is already installed and is the single motion runtime for authored sequences.
- The Vendure catalogue can be unavailable or contain starter technology fixtures; the fashion campaign must remain coherent in either state.
- Nigerian and international market routing must remain explicit in the URL.
